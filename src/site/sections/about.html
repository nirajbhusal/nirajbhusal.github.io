About

I’m a civil servant with the Government of Nepal, currently at the Office of the Hon. Finance Minister, Ministry of Finance, working at the intersection of government technology, AI governance, and digital public infrastructure (DPI).

Alongside day-to-day public service, I help surface official public records — including briefing the Finance Minister and forwarding the Rasuwa Flood Update Portal (Ministry of Finance (MoF)) to MoF IT for linking on mof.gov.np — and I build personal civic experiments such as the independent Rasuwa Flood Update Portal. I also help grow communities like GovTech Nepal.

I care about systems people can trust: accurate data, clear routing to official channels, and tools that respect the line between personal initiative and government authority.
