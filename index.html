About

I’m a civil servant with the Government of Nepal, currently at the Ministry of Finance, working at the intersection of government technology, AI governance, and digital public infrastructure (DPI).

Alongside day-to-day public service, I help surface official public records — including briefing the Finance Minister and forwarding the MoF Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi Flood Update to MoF IT for linking on mof.gov.np — and I build personal civic experiments such as the independent flood bulletin. I also help grow communities like GovTech Nepal.

I care about systems people can trust: accurate data, clear routing to official channels, and tools that respect the line between personal initiative and government authority.

Independent personal civic information site — not a government website — aggregating timestamped, fact-checked updates during the August 2026 flood response. Built with Grok; routes citizens to official rescue and donation channels. Distinct from the official MoF portal above.
