About

I’m a civil servant in Nepal working at the intersection of government technology, AI governance, and digital public infrastructure (DPI).

Day job: Assistant IT Officer at the Ministry of Finance / Financial Comptroller General Office. Side of desk: personal civic experiments — including a flood information bulletin built during the Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi crisis.

I care about systems people can trust: accurate data, clear routing to official channels, and tools that respect the line between personal initiative and government authority.
