About

I’m a civil servant with the Government of Nepal, currently at the Ministry of Finance, working at the intersection of government technology, AI governance, and digital public infrastructure (DPI).

Alongside day-to-day public service, I build personal civic experiments — including a flood information bulletin during the Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi crisis — and help grow communities like GovTech Nepal.

I care about systems people can trust: accurate data, clear routing to official channels, and tools that respect the line between personal initiative and government authority.
